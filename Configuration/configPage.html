LyricFin: Get Timed Lyrics

Fetches timed LRC lyrics from LRCLIB. The scheduled task only fills tracks that are missing lyrics.

Scheduled runs ignore titles marked (Instrumental) / [Instrumental].

Matching

Comma-separated markers stripped from track titles before lookup (same as MusicFin), e.g. 🅴 or [Explicit].

Censoring

Mask swear words when lyrics are saved. May require Force refresh.
Word list
One word per line under # Words or # Roots (Clear and press Save to restore).
Advanced
Concurrent tracks (LRCLIB prefers slow/polite clients). 0 or 1 recommended. Max 4.

Force refresh

Overwrites existing lyrics! Don't use unless you know what you're doing!
